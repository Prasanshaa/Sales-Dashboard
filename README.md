# Sales Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, and business trends through data visualization and business intelligence.

##  Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming sales data into meaningful business insights using an interactive Power BI dashboard. The dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, profit distribution, customer segments, regional trends, and shipping methods.

The objective of this project is to develop practical skills in data visualization, business reporting, and analytical problem-solving while presenting complex data in a clear and structured format.

##  Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability.
- Track key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Identify sales trends across different time periods.
- Compare regional and category-wise performance.
- Understand customer segments and payment methods.
- Present business insights through interactive visualizations.

##  Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Data Analysis** – Sales and profitability analysis
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPIs, and interactive reporting
- **CSV Dataset** – SuperStore sales data

##  Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Average Delivery

### 2. Sales & Category Analysis
- Sales performance across product categories.
- Sub-category sales comparison.
- Customer segment analysis.

### 3. Regional & Geographic Analysis
- Profit comparison across regions.
- Sales and profit distribution by state.
- Identification of regional performance patterns.

### 4. Time-Based Analysis
- Monthly sales and profit trends.
- Year-over-Year (YoY) comparison.
- Analysis of business performance over time.

### 5. Customer & Order Analysis
- Sales distribution by customer segment.
- Payment mode analysis.
- Shipping mode comparison.
